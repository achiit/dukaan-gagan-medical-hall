A counter our neighbours have known since 2011

I opened Gagan Medical Hall in 2011 with a small shelf and a big notebook. My brother Rahul joined me soon after, and I still remember our first customer, an old man who walked over from the mandir for a strip of paracetamol and stayed to chat.

Fifteen years later, many of the same families still walk in. Some come for tablets, some for a child’s syrup, some for their cattle. We try to treat all of them the way we would want our own family treated.

  • Prices are written out. You will not be asked for more at the counter.
  • Open 8 AM to 8 PM, every day of the week.
  • Two phone numbers, both on WhatsApp, answered by the owners.
  • Medicines for people and for cattle under one roof.
  • Right on the main road, opposite Devpuri Mandir.
